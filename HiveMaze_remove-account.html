HiveMaze — Account and data deletion

Application: HiveMaze · Developer: PierIngro
Last updated: 2026-09-19

This page explains how to request deletion of your HiveMaze account and its associated data, which data is deleted, which data is retained, and for how long.

How to request deletion

Send an email to whiterhombuses@gmail.com including:

  • subject: HiveMaze account deletion request;
  • the username you use to sign in to HiveMaze;
  • the display name shown on the in-game leaderboard.

You will receive a reply within 10 business days. To protect your account from third-party requests, we may ask you to confirm ownership before proceeding.

If you play without an account

HiveMaze can be played without registering: in that case your progress is tied to a random identifier generated and stored by the app on your device. Because there is no username and no password, we cannot verify that an unregistered account belongs to you and therefore cannot delete it on email request. You can remove the data held on your device by uninstalling the app; data held on our servers is deleted automatically after 6 months of inactivity.

Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove from our servers:

CategoryData
Identity Player identifier, username, password (stored only in encrypted form), display name, device identifier, account creation date
Game progress In-game currency (Cash and Tickets), node levels, upgrades, grid map, Swarms performed, Pheromones, purchased accelerator slots, active temporary boosters
Leaderboard Your rank and display name are removed from the public leaderboard
History The in-game currency transaction log

Deletion is irreversible: progress cannot be recovered once confirmed.

Data retained after deletion

DataRetentionReason
Server technical logs, which may contain the username Up to 10 days, then automatically erased Diagnostics and security; not used for any other purpose
Correspondence about the deletion request Up to 6 months Evidence that the request was fulfilled

Data handled by third parties

HiveMaze shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Data collected by Google in that context (including the device advertising identifier) is not in our possession and we cannot delete it on your behalf. You can manage it directly:

  • on your Android device: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset the advertising identifier;
  • through Google's privacy controls: myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

We do not share your username, display name or game progress with Google or any other third party.

Contact

For any question about this procedure or about how your data is handled: whiterhombuses@gmail.com.

HiveMaze is an online game: your progress is stored on my server and not on your phone, so the app has to identify your game. It does so without knowing who you are, as explained below.

Information Collection and Use

I do not ask you for your name, your email address, your phone number, or any other information that identifies you as a person. The app does not use your location, your camera, your microphone, your contacts, or your files. The only Android permission the app requests is INTERNET, which the game needs in order to reach my server.

To keep your game running, the following information is created by the app and stored on my server:

  • A random game code. The first time you open the app, it generates a random code (a UUID, a sequence such as 3f2a9c7e-…), stores it in the encrypted area of your device (Android Keystore) and sends it to my server, which uses it to recognise your game every time you play. This code is not your device identifier, it is not your advertising identifier, and it is not connected to your Google account in any way. It is invented by the app and leads back to nobody.
  • Your game progress. Cash, Tickets, Tickets earned in total, node levels, the grid and its obstacles, active boosters, the number of Swarms, and the time of your last save. This is what lets you close the app and find your factory where you left it.
  • Your display name. The game assigns you one automatically (for example «Busy Bee 42») and you can change it from the Settings screen. This name is public: it is shown to other players in the leaderboard, together with your Tickets earned and your Swarms. Please do not put personal information in it. Nothing else of yours is ever shown to other players.
  • A username and a password, only if you choose to create them. Linking an account is optional and exists for one reason: if you uninstall the app, the random game code is lost and so is access to that game. No email address is requested, and your username is never shown to other players. Your password is never stored as you typed it: my server keeps only a BCrypt hash, which cannot be turned back into the original password.

Advertising

HiveMaze shows rewarded ads only: short videos that you start yourself, by tapping a button, to receive a temporary boost in the game. There are no banners and no ads that appear on their own. If you never tap that button, you never see an advertisement.

The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show an ad, Google's software development kit inside the app processes information of its own, including your device's advertising identifier (a code you can reset or switch off in your Android settings), technical information about your device and the app, your IP address, and how you interacted with the ad. For this information Google acts as an independent controller, under its own rules: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

So that the reward is given only to somebody who really watched the video, my server does not take the app's word for it: the app passes Google two technical values — your internal game identifier and a single-use code valid for two minutes — and Google sends them back to my server together with a cryptographic signature. My server keeps those values in memory for a few minutes in order to check that signature, and never writes them to its database.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the app you are shown Google's consent form, where you choose whether to allow personalised ads and the advertising partners behind them. If you refuse, the game works exactly the same and the rewarded videos are still available: the ads are simply not personalised. You can change your mind at any time by clearing the app's data in your Android settings, which makes the consent form appear again the next time you open the app, or by turning off ad personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads on your device, where you can also reset your advertising identifier.

Why I am allowed to process this information

For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, these are the legal bases under Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b) — running the game, saving your progress, showing the leaderboard and handling the optional account. Without this information the game cannot work at all.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f) — keeping the Service secure, preventing abuse and rewards obtained by cheating, and showing non-personalised rewarded ads, which is what allows me to offer the game for free.
  • Consent (Art. 6.1.a) — showing personalised rewarded ads and sharing the related information with advertising partners. This consent is collected through Google's consent form and can be withdrawn at any time, as described above.

Log Data

I want to inform you that whenever you use my Service, my server and my hosting provider record Log Data. This Log Data may include your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, the time and date of your use of the Service, which request was made and whether it succeeded. It is used to keep the Service working and to protect it from abuse; it is not used to profile you and it is not attached to your game profile.

Unlike many apps, HiveMaze contains no analytics and no crash reporting software: nothing in the app watches how you play or reports back to me when something goes wrong. Your username, if you created one, appears in my server logs when you sign in; your password and your game code never do.
